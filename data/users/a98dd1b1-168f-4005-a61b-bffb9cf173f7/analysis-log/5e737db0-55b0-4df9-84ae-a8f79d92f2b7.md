## Analysis

### 1. Tradability & Execution Constraint
- **Unlisted Security:** Because Manipal Health is unlisted, retail capital cannot enter through standard NSE/BSE brokerage channels. 
- **Pre-IPO / Unlisted Market Risk:** While unlisted shares trade off-market, the framework advises **extreme caution** due to wide bid-ask spreads, lack of quarterly regulatory filing obligations under SEBI listing regulations, and high illiquidity.

### 2. Business Quality & Moat Benchmark
- **Network & Regional Dominance:** Manipal possesses a strong cluster moat in Southern India (Bengaluru/Karnataka) and expanded rapidly into Eastern India (via AMRI Hospitals and Medica Synergie).
- **ARPOB & Capital Intensity:** [FACT] Average Revenue Per Occupied Bed (ARPOB) is robust (~₹55,000+), driven by tertiary/quaternary care. 
- **Peer Comparison (Listed Sector Options):**
  - **Max Healthcare:** Industry leader in capital efficiency and ARPOB (~₹70,000+), zero net debt, clean corporate structure.
  - **Apollo Hospitals:** Integrated healthcare play with digital/pharmacy tailwinds.
  - **Fortis Healthcare:** Turnaround story with improving operating margins.

### 3. Valuation & PCCL
