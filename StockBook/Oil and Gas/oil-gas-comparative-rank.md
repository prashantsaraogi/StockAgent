## Weighted scoring methodology

Same **7-parameter framework** as `pharma-comparative-rank.md` and `healthcare-comparative-rank.md`.

| # | Parameter | Weight | What it measures |
|---|-----------|-------:|------------------|
| **A** | **PCCL / pessimistic margin of safety** | **25%** | Capital protection vs **stress PCCL only** |
| **B** | **Reverse portfolio allocation** | **25%** | **Higher existing O&G weight → lower score** |
| **C** | **Potential growth** (3-yr base) | **12%** | Volume, production, margin recovery |
| **D** | **Management quality** | **10%** | PSU governance, capital allocation |
| **E** | **FII / institutional backing** | **10%** | Liquidity + downside cushion |
| **F** | **Internal risk** (inverse) | **9%** | Margin, execution, balance sheet |
| **G** | **External risk** (inverse) | **9%** | Crude, LNG, policy, Delhi EV |

### A — PCCL = pessimistic margin of safety only

```
Premium to PCCL (%) = (CMP − PCCL_low) / PCCL_low × 100
Score_A             = clamp(10 − Premium_to_PCCL_low ÷ 10, 0, 10)
```

| Name | PCCL_low | CMP (28 Aug) | Premium | **Score A** |
|------|----------|-------------:|--------:|:-----------:|
| **IGL** | ₹170 | **₹151** | **−11%** | **10.0** |
| **ONGC** | ₹180 | **₹236** | **+31%** | **6.9** |
| **IOC** | ₹85 | **₹139** | **+63%** | **3.7** |

*Negative premium → capped at 10.0.*

### B — Reverse allocation (O&G sector book + total portfolio)

```
O&G sector cost ≈ ₹5.37L (IGL 81% · IOC 12% · ONGC 6%)
Blended_weight = 0.70 × (O&G_cost_share) + 0.30 × (Total_portfolio_cost_share)
Score_B        = 10 × (1 − Blended_weight)
```
