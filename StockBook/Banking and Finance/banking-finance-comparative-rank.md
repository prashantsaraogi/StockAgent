## Weighted scoring methodology

Same **7-parameter framework** as healthcare / pharma / FMCG. Uses **Applied PCCL** when holder at loss (`pccl-loss-position-floor.md`).

| # | Parameter | Weight | What it measures |
|---|-----------|-------:|------------------|
| **A** | PCCL / pessimistic MoS | **25%** | vs **Applied PCCL** |
| **B** | Reverse allocation | **25%** | Higher **private bank/finance book** weight → lower score |
| **C** | Growth (3-yr) | **12%** | Loan/deposit CAGR, PAT, NIM path |
| **D** | Management / governance | **10%** | Execution, RBI overlay, capital allocation |
| **E** | FII backing | **10%** | Level + QoQ trend (Jun 2026) |
| **F** | Internal risk (inverse) | **9%** | Asset quality, integration, fraud flags |
| **G** | External risk (inverse) | **9%** | RBI, rates, credit cycle, macro |

**Bucket for B:** Private banks + life/NBFC/AMC held in `Banking and Finance` reports — **₹26.44L** cost (ex-PSU).

```
Premium to PCCL (%) = (CMP − Applied_PCCL) / Applied_PCCL × 100
Score_A             = clamp(10 − Premium ÷ 10, 0, 10)
Blended_weight      = 0.70 × (Bucket_cost_share) + 0.30 × (Total_portfolio_cost_share)
Score_B             = 10 × (1 − Blended_weight)
```
