## 2. Structural vs crisis — Indian IT consulting

### The old model (what built the moat)

```
Large fresher base → train on bench → T&M billing × headcount × utilisation
Client pays for "effort"; Indian IT wins on scale + cost + execution discipline
```

### The new model (2026 reality)

```
AI + agents → fewer people per unit of output
Clients demand productivity in RFPs ("show gain in price or timeline")
Revenue decoupling from headcount — TCS CC growth weak while rupee revenue up
AI revenue run rate rising — but may **replace** not **add** traditional services
```

| Test | Crisis (temporary) | Structural (permanent) |
|------|-------------------|------------------------|
| Will demand return to old headcount growth? | — | **Unlikely** at prior pyramid depth |
| Is moat intact? | Partially — delivery scale remains | **Pricing power on commoditised coding erodes** |
| Substitute gaining share? | — | **AI-assisted delivery + GCC insourcing** |
| Duration | — | **Multi-year rewrite** |

**Verdict:** **Transitional with structural permanence on the labour pyramid** — not existential for top-tier Indian IT, but **lower sustainable growth + margin pressure on legacy T&M**.

**Severity:** **Moderate–High** on **entry-level / commoditised coding**  
**Permanence:** **Permanent** for pyramid shape; **Transitional** for total industry revenue (still growing low-single-digit CC)

## 3. Threat path (how AI hits Indian IT economics)

```
GenAI + agentic tools in SDLC
  → Client expects 20–40% productivity pass-through in contracts (RFP trend — AIM/Faros)
  → Billable headcount per $ revenue falls
  → Fresher intake collapses; lateral AI-skilled hiring rises
  → Short term: margins hold via utilisation + AI-led deals
  → Medium term: revenue growth ≠ headcount growth (already visible at TCS/Infosys)
  → Pricing on "lines of code" commoditises; value shifts to:
        • AI deployment & governance
        • Domain + transformation
        • Quality / security / compliance (your QA world)
        • Forward-deployed engineers (TCS targeting 5,900–8,900)
```

### The "AI quality paradox" — **opportunity for QA professionals**

| Phenomenon | Implication for QA |
|------------|-------------------|
| Faster code generation | **More** change volume → **more** test demand |
| Higher defect / incident rates with AI code | **Human validation** becomes bottleneck |
| Enterprise compliance (BFSI, healthcare) | Cannot ship AI code without audit trail + governance |
| Sembi 2026: **61%** report higher QA demand from AI code | **Validation is the new constraint** |

**Your worry about bugs is not a bug in your career — it is the industry’s new billable problem.**

Roles growing: **SDET, Quality Engineer, AI test governance, LLM eval, red-teaming, shift-left automation**  
Roles shrinking: **pure manual regression at bottom of pyramid**

| QA skill (2026) | Demand |
|-----------------|--------|
| Playwright / API / CI automation | **Table stakes** |
| AI-assisted test design + reviewing AI output | **Premium (+15–25% pay)** |
| Risk-based exploratory testing | **Critical** — AI weak on edge cases |
| Domain knowledge (BFSI, healthcare ERP) | **Moat** for Indian QA in enterprise |

## 4. Indian IT as national employment engine

| Metric | Reading | Implication |
|--------|---------|-------------|
| Total tech workforce | **~5.9M** (Nasscom 2026) | Still huge — not collapsing |
| Net headcount top-5 FY26 | **−6,981** | Selective shrink, not mass layoffs |
| Q1 FY27 rebound | **+5,400** (TCS +9k) | Cyclical + project hiring, not pyramid reset |
| Fresher hiring | **−80%** from FY22 peak | **Structural** — hurts campus pipeline, not today’s mid/senior QA |
| GCC hiring | Growing share of tech jobs | Jobs stay in India but **shift employer type** |
| GDP linkage | IT exports ~$200B+; urban middle-class anchor | **Slowdown hurts sentiment & consumption** — your macro worry is valid |

**Macro read:** A **sharp IT employment crash** would ripple through India — but **2026 evidence is "reshape" not "collapse"**. Risk is **gradual compression of entry-level opportunity** + **wage pressure on commoditised roles**, not overnight mass unemployment.
