## 6. Fair P/E range — India perspective (not anchored to today's multiple)

**Question:** What P/E *should* Maruti trade at in India — independent of where it is today?

### Why P/E is not one number for Maruti

Maruti is **not** a consumer staple (HUL-style 50–60×) nor a deep cyclical (Ashok Leyland 12–18× at trough). It sits in a **third bucket**:

| Bucket | India examples | Typical fair P/E | Maruti fit |
|--------|------------------|------------------:|------------|
| Deep cyclical OEM | Ashok Leyland, CV cycle trough | 12–20× | No — scale + net cash |
| Quality volume OEM | Hero, Bajaj (2W) | 20–24× | Close — but Maruti **lower ROE** |
| **Dominant PV franchise** | **Maruti, Hyundai India** | **24–30×** | **Yes** |
| Premium niche | Eicher (Royal Enfield) | 28–38× | Partial — RE premium, not full PV |
| Euphoria / low-rate peak | Maruti Mar-2022 | 45–60× | **Not fair** — cycle + liquidity |

### What drives Maruti's fair multiple in India

| Driver | Maruti reading | P/E impact | Type |
|--------|----------------|------------|------|
| **PV market share** | ~41–43% (FADA May 2026; record Apr FY27) | +3–5 pp vs peer | FACT |
| **ROE** | ~14–15% (borderline vs 15% compounder gate) | Caps above **30×** unless ROE rises | FACT |
| **EPS growth (5Y)** | **8–12%** base (volume + SUV mix; margin normalisation) | Core anchor **24–28×** | ASSUMPTION |
| **Cyclicality** | Medium — SIAM, rural, oil, rates | −2–4 pp vs “defensive” | FACT |
| **Balance sheet** | Net cash, Altman safe | +2 pp vs leveraged OEM | FACT |

| **Capex wave** | ₹77,500 cr FY27–31 (AGM Aug 2026) | ROE drag → **lower** fair P/E vs past peaks | FACT |
| **India PV penetration** | Under-penetrated vs China/US; GDP tailwind | +2 pp in up-cycle | ASSUMPTION |

### Three methods — converging range

**Method A — ROE × P/B (Indian auto norm)**  
Fair P/B for 14–15% ROE auto leader ≈ **3.0–3.8×**.  
Implied P/E = P/B ÷ ROE → **22–27×** (base **~25×**).

**Method B — Growth + quality premium (India retail market)**  
EPS CAGR **10%** + quality/moat premium **12–15 pp** → **22–25×** conservative, **26–28×** normal.  
Maruti’s #1 share justifies **upper half** of band vs Tata PV/Hyundai.

**Method C — Own history (what India has *paid*, not what it *should*)**  
10Y median ~**28×** · 5Y avg ~**38×** (distorted by 2022 peak **~59×**) · trough Mar-2025 **~25×**.  
**Forward fair** should be **below** 10Y average because: (1) ROE not at 18–20% pre-COVID peaks, (2) heavy capex, (3) EV uncertainty.

### Verdict — fair P/E bands for Maruti (India)

| Band | P/E range | When it applies | Investor read |
|------|----------:|-----------------|---------------|
| **Deep value / PCCL** | **20–22×** | Cycle scare, margin collapse, oil >$100 sustained | Pessimistic stress — rare in normal markets |
| **Attractive / steady SIP** | **22–26×** | Normal margins, 10%+ volume, share stable | **Primary fair zone** for long-term India holder |
| **Full fair / quality premium** | **26–30×** | SUV mix working, ROE →15%+, SIAM strong | Acceptable for **legacy low-cost** book; token pace for fresh |
| **Expensive** | **30–35×** | Growth priced in; ROE still ~14% | Slow SIP only — earnings must grow into multiple |
| **Rich / euphoria** | **>35×** | Liquidity, narrative, not fundamentals | **Not** sustainable fair — seen 2021–22 in India |

**Centre of gravity (normalised earnings, through-cycle):** **24–28×**, with **26×** as the best single “fair” anchor for India today.

**Not justified long-term:** **>32×** unless ROE sustainably **≥16%** and EPS CAGR **≥14%** with EV execution proof — **HYPOTHESIS** until Q2 FY27+ margins confirm.
