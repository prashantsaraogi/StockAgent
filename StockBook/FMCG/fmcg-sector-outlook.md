## Ranking — 3–5 year business trajectory

### 🥇 1. Tata Consumer — best growth story, biggest valuation risk

**Why trajectory is strongest:** Delivering the **volume growth** that can justify a premium multiple. Growth businesses reached **31%** of India business in FY26; India branded UVG **13%** (FACT).

**But:** Already carries a **high valuation hurdle**. Future returns depend on **EPS catching up** with the growth narrative.

**Rule (OUR ASSUMPTION):**

| EPS path | P/E implication |
|----------|-----------------|
| **15%+ EPS CAGR** | Premium P/E can **survive** |
| EPS falls toward **8–10%** | **Major P/E derating** risk |

### 🥈 2. HUL — best quality, waiting for growth proof

Watch most closely for **re-rating from operational improvement**.

- FY26 UVG **4%**, but investment rising — capex intensity toward **3% of turnover**, premiumisation, future channels (MANAGEMENT CLAIM / FACT mix).
- FY26 ROCE **110.9%** (FACT) — exceptional.
- **Key question:** Can HUL move from **4–6%** to **sustainable 6–8%+** volume growth **without destroying margins**?
  - **Yes** → **35–45×** defendable; **50×+** possible in strong cycle.
  - **No**, EPS **6–8%** → market questions **certainty premium**.

### 🥉 3. ITC — best valuation comfort (lowest P/E requirement)

**Fundamentally different** from HUL/Tata Consumer — earnings mix includes **cigarettes, agri, paper**; regulatory risk in largest cash generator.

Should **not** trade at HUL/Tata-type multiples. Valuation can still work via **EPS growth + dividend yield + cash generation**.

**Care about:** EPS growth + YoC + cash — **not** P/E expansion.

**20–30× sustainable P/E is not negative** for ITC — can still produce good shareholder returns.
