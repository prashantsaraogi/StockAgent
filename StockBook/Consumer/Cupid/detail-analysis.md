## 1. Business model & moat

**How it makes money:** Manufacturer and exporter of male/female condoms, water-based lubricants, and in-vitro diagnostic (IVD) kits. Nashik-based plant; **~85% revenue from international B2B** (UNFPA, WHO pre-qualified supplier lists, government health programmes). Domestic consumer healthcare / FMCG expansion via retail (~1.5 lakh+ outlets cited in company materials — MANAGEMENT CLAIM).

| Moat element | Evidence | Rating |
|--------------|----------|--------|
| Regulatory pre-qualification (WHO/UNFPA) | Long-standing exporter credentials | **Moderate** — replicable with capital/time |
| Export order book | Q1 FY27 scale + upgraded FY27 guide | **Moderate** — customer concentration risk |
| Brand (Cupid) in condoms | Niche; not HUL-scale consumer | **Low–Moderate** |
| Cost / margin | EBITDA ~39% Q1 FY27 | **Strong** — FACT |
| Switching costs | Institutional tenders | **Moderate** |

**Why competitors cannot easily replicate:** Pre-qualification + audit trail + scale at 39% margins create friction, but this is **not** a wide consumer moat like Nestlé/HUL. Moat is **B2B institutional + execution**, not pricing power with end consumers.

**Classification:** Real moat in **export niche** · Management narrative on ₹1,500 cr FY29 is **ASSUMPTION** until repeated in filings.
