# Industry Business Model Templates

Use these when analyzing companies in the listed sectors.
All metrics must come from verified sources — do not invent values.

## Asset Management Company (AMC / Mutual Fund)

### How the business works

An AMC earns fees by managing investors' money pooled into mutual
fund schemes. Revenue is primarily:

```
Fee income ≈ Average AUM × Fee yield (TER/BER in bps)
```

Secondary revenue: portfolio management services (PMS), alternatives
(AIF), offshore/GIFT City funds, advisory, treasury income on float.

The AMC does **not** own the underlying securities — it manages them
and charges a management fee.

### Revenue drivers

| Driver | What to check |
|--------|---------------|
| QAAUM / AUM | Quarterly/monthly average and closing AUM |
| Market share | % of industry QAAUM |
| Fee yield | Overall bps; equity vs debt vs liquid vs ETF |
| Product mix | Active equity vs passive/index vs debt vs liquid |
| Channel mix | Direct vs regular (MFD/distributor) |
| Customer mix | Retail vs HNI vs institutional |
| SIP book | Monthly SIP inflows and count |
| Unique investors | Count and penetration vs industry |
| B-30 penetration | Tier-2/3 city contribution |

### Cost structure

| Cost | Notes |
|------|-------|
| Distributor commission | Largest variable; affected by direct-plan shift |
| People | Fund managers, sales, operations |
| Technology | Platforms, compliance systems |
| Marketing | Investor acquisition |
| Compliance / regulatory | SEBI, audit, governance |

AMCs are **asset-light** — high operating margins (60–80%+) at scale.
Operating leverage is strong: costs grow slower than AUM.

### Key KPIs to pull (FACT only)

- QAAUM and YoY growth
- Closing AUM
- Market share (total and active equity)
- Equity : non-equity AUM ratio
- Fee yield (bps) — overall and by category
- Unique investors / live accounts
- SIP contribution (company and industry)
- Direct vs regular AUM split
- Active vs passive market share
- Alternatives AUM (PMS, AIF)
- OPM (operating profit margin)
- PAT margin on revenue

### Moat evidence (not narrative)

- Stable or rising market share over 3–5 years
- High unique-investor penetration (e.g. 25%+ of industry)
- Strong SIP book (sticky, recurring inflows)
- Brand trust during market downturns (lower redemption intensity)
- Distribution reach (empaneled partners, B-30 offices)
- Performance track record across fund categories

### Structural risks

| Risk | Impact |
|------|--------|
| SEBI fee (TER/BER) cuts | Lower fee yield |
| Passive / ETF shift | Lower bps on index funds |
| Direct plan growth | Cuts distributor commission but may improve AMC margin |
| Equity market correction | AUM and fee income fall (lag effect) |
| Competition from bank-owned AMCs | Market share pressure |
| Star fund manager departure | Outflows from flagship schemes |
| Regulatory overhaul | SEBI MF Regulations, performance-linked fees |

### Peer set (India)

HDFC AMC, ICICI Prudential AMC, SBI MF, Nippon India AMC,
Kotak MF, UTI AMC, Axis AMC, Mirae Asset, DSP, etc.

For **listed** AMC stocks: compare QAAUM growth, market share,
fee yield, OPM, and ROE across peers.

### Early warning signals

- Market share down 2+ consecutive quarters
- Fee yield declining without mix explanation
- SIP market share falling
- Rising expense ratio without AUM growth
- Key scheme underperformance vs benchmark (3Y/5Y)
- Net outflows in equity schemes

## Auto — Two-Wheeler / PV / CV

**Revenue:** Units × ASP; exports separate
**Monthly KPIs:** SIAM industry sales; company dispatches (domestic + export)
**Moat:** Brand, distribution, service network, cost platform
**Risks:** Cycle, EV transition, discounting, rural income, financing rates

Use `dynamic-context-analysis/sector-monthly-kpis.md` for monthly tracking
(e.g. Hero MotoCorp vs SIAM 2W trend). Do not rely on quarterly alone.

## Bank / NBFC (Brief)

**Revenue:** NII (spread) + fee income
**KPIs:** NIM, CASA ratio, GNPA/NNPA, PCR, credit growth, ROA, ROE
**Moat:** Deposit franchise, CASA, distribution, underwriting discipline
**Risks:** Credit cycle, ALM, regulation, competition

---

## Pharmaceutical (Brief)

**Revenue:** Branded/generic sales by geography and therapy
**KPIs:** US/API/ex-India mix, ANDA count, R&D spend, gross margin
**Moat:** Regulated approvals, distribution, complex generics
**Risks:** USFDA, pricing pressure, patent cliffs, currency

---

## IT Services (Brief)

**Revenue:** Billable headcount × rate × utilization
**KPIs:** CC growth, EBIT margin, attrition, deal TCV, client concentration
**Moat:** Client relationships, domain expertise, delivery track record
**Risks:** Disruption, pricing, visa/regulation, AI impact

## Generic Template (Any Sector)

When no template exists, fill in:

1. **Customer** — who pays
2. **Product** — what they buy
3. **Pricing** — how price is set
4. **Volume driver** — what scales revenue
5. **Unit economics** — revenue and cost per unit if applicable
6. **Moat** — evidence-based
7. **Top 3 peers** — compare same KPIs
8. **Top 3 model risks** — with leading indicators
