# PCCL as Protection — Not a Practical Buy Target

Use with `pccl-analysis/SKILL.md`, `premium-add-sizing.md`,
`salary-systematic-accumulation.md`, and `long-term-investor-mandate.md`.

Implements user doctrine (Aug 2026): **PCCL protects; upward scale-in builds wealth.**

---

## Core doctrine

**PCCL is a pessimistic stress limit and sizing anchor — not a price
you should expect to buy at in normal markets.**

For **Strong** compounders (e.g. Max Healthcare, ICICI Bank, LT) in
structurally growing sectors:

- Price **stays above PCCL** most of the time when thesis is intact
- **Systematic scale-in above PCCL** (salary DCA, fair-value adds with
  size caps) is how long-term holders **build quantity and wealth**
- PCCL **protects** — it caps add size, quantifies new-tranche risk,
  and defines the zone for **accelerated** buying if a rare dislocation occurs

```
Wealth ≈ Quantity × Time × Business quality
```

Waiting years for PCCL on a quality winner **forfeits quantity** and
is **not** the primary wealth path for existing holders.

**Continuous SIP (Aug 2026 user doctrine):** Add **every month** in good shares
at **tier-appropriate pace** — even at premium. Sideways markets + rising earnings
mean timing is unknowable; PCCL + blended cap **protect**, they do not block SIP.

See **`continuous-sip-valuation-tiers.md`** for five-tier pace table.

## When PCCL is realistically reachable

PCCL (or deep approach toward it) typically requires **abnormal** conditions:

| Trigger class | Examples |
|---------------|----------|
| **Market / sector crash** | COVID-style panic, broad Nifty −25%+ drawdown, sector-specific scare |
| **Company-specific crisis** | Governance failure, fraud allegation proven, existential legal loss, major earnings collapse |
| **Core-problem materialisation** | Moat break, regulatory destruction of economics, management inability to execute |
| **Rare valuation washout** | Multi-quarter derating when earnings temporarily weak *and* sentiment breaks |

**Normal conditions** (steady GDP, intact thesis, PAT lagging revenue,
sector competition like "mushrooming hospitals") → price may **compress
toward** PCCL but **not touch it for years** — or never, if earnings
grow into the multiple.

**Do not** present PCCL as a **base-case 3-year price target** or imply
that patient waiting at cash until PCCL is the default plan for
scale winners the user already holds at favourable cost.

## Two types of "averaging" (never confuse)

| Pattern | Definition | Framework |
|---------|------------|-----------|
| **Fair-value scale-in above PCCL** | Scheduled or thesis-driven adds at CMP when MoS vs **Conservative IV** ≥ 0%, with **premium-to-PCCL size caps** | **Primary wealth path** for long-term holders |
| **Momentum / FOMO averaging** | Larger adds because price rose, stock is "hot", or unrealized gain feels good — **without** price rules or caps | **Avoid** |

User term **"upward averaging"** = **fair-value scale-in above PCCL**
(first row), **not** momentum chasing (second row).

## What PCCL actually controls

| Role | How PCCL is used |
|------|------------------|
| **Size cap** | Premium to PCCL → max monthly/lump add (see `premium-add-sizing.md`) |
| **New-tranche risk** | Quantify loss on **fresh** capital if price revisits PCCL |
| **Legacy protection** | Blended avg vs Applied PCCL — old shares may still be green at stress PCCL even when Applied = CMP on loss book |
| **Accelerate zone** | If price **approaches or breaches** PCCL → run core-problem test; **increase** pace if thesis intact |
| **Fresh capital rank** | Names **closer to PCCL** rank higher for **new** positions with no legacy cost |
| **Pause signal** | Price **above Conservative IV** → pause or token only — not sell |

PCCL does **not** mean: *"Never buy until ₹X."*

### Meaningful premium to PCCL (buy zone above PCCL)

Adds happen **above PCCL** in normal markets. The ceiling is **not** a fixed
premium % — derive it from **latest results + Conservative IV**:

```
Conservative IV = Normalized EPS (mid-cycle) × fair P/E
Meaningful premium ceiling % = (Conservative IV − PCCL) / PCCL × 100
```

| Zone vs Conservative IV | Add stance |
|-------------------------|------------|
| CMP **≤ IV** (MoS vs IV ≥ 0%) | **Full scale-in** allowed — premium to PCCL may be **large** if PCCL is set low |
| CMP **0–8% above IV** | **Slow / token** DCA only |
| CMP **> 8% above IV** | **Pause** new adds — HOLD legacy |

**Always tie to latest quarter:** if revenue grows ~15–26% but PAT only ~0–5%
(hospital PAT lag), do **not** raise IV aggressively — caps meaningful premium.

Re-run when next quarterly results publish.

## Agent output rules

When user is a **long-term holder** of a **Strong** compounder:

1. **State PCCL** and premium to PCCL — always
2. **Do not** imply PCCL is likely within 1–3 years unless core-problem or crash scenario is explicit
3. **Recommend salary DCA / scale-in above PCCL** when MoS vs IV ≥ 0%, thesis intact, size caps applied
4. **Separate** 3-year **EPS growth projection** (upside path) from **PCCL** (downside protection) — different purposes
5. For **fresh capital** with **no position**, premium to PCCL still matters for **rank vs alternatives** — waiting is more acceptable than for existing holders

### Example language (Max Healthcare pattern)

- **Wrong:** "Wait for PCCL ₹750 before adding."
- **Right:** "PCCL ₹750 is your **protection floor** (+33% premium at CMP). Normal wealth path: **size-capped salary DCA at ₹1,000**; **accelerate** below ₹850; **full pace** only if price approaches ₹750 **and** core-problem test passes."

## Anti-patterns (never)

- Treat PCCL as **expected** buy price for quality compounders in base case
- Block **all** adds on scale winners solely because premium to PCCL > 0
- Confuse **fair-value scale-in above PCCL** with **momentum chasing**
- Present downward "drift to PCCL" as practical path without crash/crisis qualifier
- Use PCCL alone to recommend **TRIM/SELL** on legacy winners (see long-term investor mandate)
