# Sector Monthly KPI Templates

Use with `dynamic-context-analysis/SKILL.md`.

Track **latest month + 3–6 month trend**. Mark all figures FACT or UNVERIFIED.
State **date checked** and **source**.

---

## Auto — Two-Wheeler (e.g. Hero MotoCorp, Bajaj Auto, TVS)

| KPI | Source | Frequency |
|-----|--------|-----------|
| SIAM industry 2W domestic sales | SIAM press release | Monthly |
| Company domestic dispatches | BSE/NSE filing or press release | Monthly |
| Company exports | Company release | Monthly |
| Industry vs company growth (YoY, MoM) | Calculate from above | Monthly |
| EV 2W industry sales (if relevant) | SIAM / FADA | Monthly |
| Average selling price trend | Concall / hypothesis | Quarterly + channel |

**Seasonality:** Festive (Sep–Nov), monsoon weakness (Jul–Aug), year-end.

**Connect:** Dispatches → revenue (lag 0–1 month) → margin (mix, discounts).

**Peer compare:** Hero vs Bajaj vs TVS market share trend over 6 months.

## Data Source Priority

1. SIAM, AMFI, RBI, DGCA, exchange filings (primary)
2. Company press releases / BSE NSE announcements
3. Reputable financial news with cited primary source
4. Research houses — HYPOTHESIS unless primary cited
5. Social media — UNVERIFIED unless confirmed
