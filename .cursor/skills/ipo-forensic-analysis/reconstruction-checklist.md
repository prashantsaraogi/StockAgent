## Notes for New-Age IPOs (Paytm, Eternal, etc.)

These listings often show:

- High OFS / VC monetisation
- Valuation on future earnings, not current cash flow
- Lock-in cliffs creating supply overhang
- Regulatory model risk (fintech, food delivery, payments)
- SBC dilution from ESOP programs

Reconstruct **IPO valuation → promoter/VC ownership → lock-in
expiry → actual selling → management exits → subsequent
earnings/regulatory events** before concluding whether a
"systematic game" hypothesis survives the numbers.
